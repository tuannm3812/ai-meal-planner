How your meal is sized

  1. A trained model estimates the energy you burn in a day.
  2. Your goal sets the calories for this meal, with a safe floor when losing weight.
  3. Portions are resized so protein, carbs and fat sit inside recommended ranges.
  4. Nutrition is checked against food data, and anything estimated is labelled.

# Slide deck design history

Why rules changed. The current rules are in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). Newest first.

## 6 November 2026

- **The cover became image-led, and the author's name moved to the close.** Mike wants the app to
  lead. Question, search bar, an illustrated labelled plate and a receipt replace the name and
  tagline, and the QR code shrinks to the bottom left.
- **The plate on a calendar replaced the plate mark for the showcase.** Mike chose it from seven
  options because it says meal planner directly. The app keeps the old mark for now.
- **Garden green on white replaced the `ground` canvas.** Mike chose theme A from four trials. Green
  titles replace ink titles, and #EEF4EF panels are added.
- **Google Sans replaced Bricolage Grotesque and Inter for the showcase.** Mike finds it more
  professional for portfolio viewers. It also matches his other decks. The app keeps its fonts.
- **Strict slide standard adopted.** Mike asked for the discipline of his Project 15 and
  Text-to-SQL decks. Their grid, type roles, takeaway columns, markers, notes pattern and
  staged workflow carry over. Kitchen green, Bricolage Grotesque and Inter stay, because
  those are this product's approved identity. Replaced the slide row in the root `DESIGN.md`
  (30–32 pt titles, 14–18 pt body, 10-minute talk).
- **Charts drawn from shapes.** A native PowerPoint chart lost its category labels when
  converted to Google Slides, showing 1 to 4. Bars are now native shapes.
- **A 14-slide live deck replaced the first 12-slide one**, built to this standard while nobody had edited the first.
- **The live deck became the source of truth** once converted. `build_deck.js` is the seed.
- **Calorie claim corrected.** The first deck said the trained model predicts daily burn and
  sets the meal target. It predicts exercise calories. The claim rule is now in DESIGN.md.

## 7 November 2026

Slides 7 to 13 moved from plain panels to the four feature colours, Material Symbols icons and to-scale figures drawn from shapes: a day-and-meal bar, a recipe funnel, a calorie-source bar, plan dots on the macro bands and a 72-dot request grid. Mike asked for clearer visuals and balanced colour.

Mike then asked for a clear architecture diagram with tech stack logos, limits on their own slide, and pending tasks. The deck is now 16 slides, and tool logos are allowed on the architecture slide only.
